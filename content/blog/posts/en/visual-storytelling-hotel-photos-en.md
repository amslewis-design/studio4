Visual content should not be decorative. It should guide decisions.

When a hotel website presents atmosphere, context, and editorial rhythm, guests can picture the stay before they commit. That sense of certainty increases direct-booking intent.

- Use narrative sequences, not disconnected shots.
- Mix wide scenes with tactile service details.
- End each section with a clear next action.
